The Need

Families in Morris County often struggle to afford the "extras." For a child, a birthday cake isn't an extra—it's a core memory.

groups

The Squad

We're a group of dedicated high school and middle school students passionate about community and the power of a simple, joyful gesture.

The Goal

To ensure no family dealing with food insecurity has to choose between a meal and a birthday celebration for their child.

FAQ

Our kits are distributed to food pantries in Morris County, NJ, including Nourish NJ and Mutual Morris. We drop off numbers of batches based on the pantries' needs.
Every kit contains boxed cake mix, frosting, oil/egg substitute (soda), candles, balloons, and a blank card. We ensure everything is shelf-stable for pantry storage.
